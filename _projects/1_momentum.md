Built upon the foundations of larp, MMORPG, urban exploration and alternate reality gaming Prosopopeia Bardo 2: Momentum was a pervasive game about conformity and revolution. The aim was to explore the borderlands between real and ludic, exploring the design space where reality and fiction merge in a seamless, immersive and coherent role-playing experience.
